01The verification gap

Once waste is collected, it leaves no record.

Waste passes through collectors, transporters, processors, recyclers and fuel buyers, each keeping separate records. Weight is measured at the gate, then the material leaves the record. The abatement technologies exist. The evidence that they were applied does not.

03Event records

Every movement is an event. Nothing is overwritten.

ADEX records each movement or treatment of waste as one event: which batch, how much it weighed, who handed it to whom and when, the evidence, and who approved it. Events that share a batch form a chain from collection to final destination. Corrections are appended as new events that reference the original.

04Mass balance

In must equal out.

For every site and period, ADEX checks that the weight that came in equals the products, residues, moisture change and documented process loss that went out, within a set tolerance. Any gap is flagged for investigation before it becomes a dispute.

06Alternative-fuel marketplace

Replace coal with verified fuel.

The marketplace exists first to cut emissions; the economic value follows from verified reductions. It matches quality-verified refuse-derived fuel (RDF) with cement kilns, coal-fired power stations and industrial boilers, and ranks every transaction by its verified carbon saving.

1List

A processor lists an RDF batch with its full ADEX history and laboratory results: calorific value, moisture, chlorine, ash and sulphur.

2Match

Cement plants, power stations and other industrial consumers state their fuel requirements. Every match shows its expected carbon saving.

3Prove

Weighbridge confirmation and buyer acceptance close the deal, and ADEX computes the coal displaced and the CO2 avoided against a declared baseline.

~8%of global CO2 comes from cement, roughly 40% of it from burning fuel in kilns.
⅓of the world's electricity still came from coal in 2025: 10,476 TWh.
~100 Mtof CO2 a year could be avoided if global cement kilns reached 22% alternative fuel (order-of-magnitude estimate).
Conservative by design.Plastic-derived RDF is not carbon neutral, so its fossil carbon is counted against the coal it displaces. Boilers tolerate lower RDF shares than kilns because of chlorine and ash, so substitution limits are set per consumer.

08Why now

The money is there. The evidence isn't. Yet.

Landfills are now visible from space.Satellite monitoring attributes some of the world's largest methane sources to landfills. Waste-sector measures could cut 13 Mt of methane a year by 2030.
Cement needs fuel that isn't coal.Kilns run below 10% alternative fuel against a 22% target for 2030. Buyers will take waste-derived fuel only if its quality and origin are proven.
Proof is becoming mandatory.Extended producer responsibility rules require evidence of recovery, not promises. The EPR market is set to double to $14 billion by 2032.
Credits need audited tonnes.Carbon and EPR markets reject claims they cannot audit. Most waste sites still run on paper and spreadsheets.
Waste is outgrowing the system.Municipal waste is on track to rise from 2.1 to 3.8 billion tonnes a year by 2050. Every extra tonne needs a record.
