05Why now

Regulation, markets and methane just lined up.

Proof is now mandatory.Extended producer responsibility rules require evidence of recovery, not promises.
Methane is the fast lever.Cutting methane is the quickest way to slow near-term warming, and waste is where it starts.
Greenhouse gases, removed.Biomining and aerobic composting stop methane, CO2 and other greenhouse gases at the source, measured tonne by tonne.
Waste replaces coal.Non-recyclables become RDF, an alternative fuel for cement kilns and waste-to-energy plants, cutting coal use and the CO2, SO2 and NOx it releases.
Buyers want audited data.Credit markets now demand measured, traceable tonnes. Most sites still run on paper and spreadsheets.
