We want everyone to be able to use advitiaum.com, and we aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

Settings you can change

Open from the header or footer of any page to:

  • make text larger (two steps);
  • switch on high contrast;
  • stop animations, including the moving headline;
  • underline every link;
  • widen letter, word and line spacing;
  • use a plain sans-serif font;
  • read the site in another language (machine translation by Google).

Your choices are saved in your browser and apply on every page. If your device is set to reduce motion, the site respects that automatically.

What we have done

  • Every page can be used with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to content" link.
  • Headings, landmarks and form labels are marked up so screen readers can navigate them, and the animated headline has a text alternative.
  • Charts and figures come with text that states the same information.
  • The layout works from phone width up, and the page can be zoomed to 200% without losing content.
  • Form errors are announced and describe how to fix them.

Known limitations

  • The white paper PDF may not be fully tagged for screen readers yet. Write to us and we will send an accessible version.
  • Some interactive diagrams are easier to read visually. The information in them is also given in the surrounding text.
  • Machine translation can break the meaning of technical terms.

Feedback

If something on the site is hard to use, tell us at {{ email }}, with the page and what happened. We aim to reply within 5 working days and, where we can, to send you the information in another format in the meantime.
